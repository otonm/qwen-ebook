<objective>
Prove — on the real RX 9070 XT / ROCm hardware — that a Transformers `StoppingCriteria`, passed through `qwen_tts`'s `generate_custom_voice(**kwargs)`, actually aborts a live decode loop promptly. This is the highest-risk unknown in the milestone (STATE.md flags it MEDIUM confidence, unverified against hardware). Land the minimal cancellation machinery in `tts_service/model.py` (it becomes the production mechanism, not throwaway), then run a hardware behavioral spike that measures time-from-cancel-to-actual-stop, and STOP at a blocking checkpoint so the measured result reaches the user before any further work assumes it works.

Purpose: De-risk D-01/D-02 before building the backend cancel plumbing and UI on top of the assumption. Per CONTEXT.md D-02, if the spike shows StoppingCriteria does NOT abort (or aborts too slowly to call "immediate"), the executor MUST halt at the checkpoint and surface findings to the user — it MUST NOT silently substitute the rejected best-effort/orphan-discard fallback.
Output: `_cancel_event` + `_CancelStoppingCriteria` + `request_cancel()` + `GenerationCancelled` in `model.py`; a standalone `spike_cancel_hw.py` timing script (mirrors the existing `smoke_gpu.py` pattern); a fast CPU unit test of the wiring; a recorded hardware measurement approved by the user.
</objective>

  </action>
